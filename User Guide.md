## 1. What is NexTill?

**NexTill** is a complete, fast, and easy-to-use billing and retail management system designed for your store. 

With NexTill, you can:
- **Ring up customer bills quickly** using a barcode scanner or simple typing.
- **Sell items by pieces, weight (kg, grams), or volume (liters, ml)**.
- **Collect cash payments** with automatic change calculation so your cash drawer is always accurate.
- **Print neat receipts** for your customers with one click.
- **Track inventory** so you never run out of your best-selling items.
- **See your daily sales, profit, and customer loyalty points**.

## 2. Getting Started & Logging In

### How to Log In:
1. Open the NexTill application on your computer or browser.
2. Enter your **Username** and **Password**.
3. Click **Sign In**.

### User Roles Explained:
NexTill has three simple levels of access:
- **Cashier**: Can bill customers, search products, print receipts, and view their own shift sales.
- **Manager**: Can do everything a cashier does, plus add new products, restock inventory, process refunds, and view all sales.
- **Admin (Store Owner)**: Has complete access, including store settings (store name, phone number, address on receipt), staff accounts, and business profit reports.

## 3. Day-to-Day Billing (Cashier POS)

The **POS (Billing)** screen is where your cashier will spend most of their day. It is designed to be lightning-fast.

### Step-by-Step Selling Flow:

```
[ Scan Barcode or Search ] ➔ [ Add Item to Bill ] ➔ [ Enter Quantity ] ➔ [ Click Charge (F2) ] ➔ [ Enter Cash & Return Change ] ➔ [ Print Receipt ]
```

#### Step 3.1: Find the Product
You have three easy ways to find an item:
- **Barcode Scanner**: Just scan the barcode on the product packaging. It will be added to the bill immediately!
- **Search Bar (Press F1)**: Type the product name or code (for example: `Rice` or `Milk`). The list will filter instantly.
- **Category Buttons**: Click on category tabs (like *Beverages*, *Groceries*, *Snacks*) to see all items in that group.

#### Step 3.2: Enter the Quantity
Each product added to the bill will show up in your cart on the right side:
- **Type the quantity freely**: Click the box (or press the down arrow) and type the number directly (e.g. `2`, `5`, `10`). The box starts clean without forcing you to delete extra numbers.
- **Use the `+` and `−` buttons**:
  - Click `+` to increase by 1.
  - Click `−` to decrease by 1. The software will never allow the quantity to drop below zero.
- **Selling by Weight or Liquid**:
  - For items like rice, vegetables, or oil, you can easily switch between units (for example, click `g` for grams or `kg` for kilograms, or `ml` for milliliters and `L` for liters).
  - You can enter decimal quantities like `1.5 kg` or `0.25 kg` (250 grams).

#### Step 3.3: Giving Discounts (Optional)
- **Item Discount**: Next to each product, there is a `Disc:` box. Type any discount amount you wish to give for that single product (e.g. `5.00`). NexTill ensures the discount never exceeds the price of the item.
- **Total Bill Discount**: At the bottom of the cart, you can enter a discount for the entire bill.

#### Step 3.4: Cash Payment & Change Calculation
1. Click the green **Charge** button (or press **F2** on your keyboard).
2. The payment window will appear showing the total bill.
3. Enter the cash amount handed by the customer:
   - *Example*: Total bill is `850.00` and customer hands you `1,000.00`. Type `1000`.
   - *Quick Button*: If the customer gave the exact amount, just click the **Exact Cash** button.
4. NexTill instantly displays the **Change** in green text: `Change: 150.00`.
5. Click **Confirm Payment** (or press **Enter**).

#### Step 3.5: Printing the Receipt
- As soon as payment is confirmed, the **Receipt** appears on screen.
- Click **Print Receipt** (or press `Ctrl + P`).
- The receipt prints on your thermal receipt printer. The numbers are clean, easy to read, and free of confusing clutter.
- Hand the receipt and change to the customer. You are ready for the next customer!

## 4. Fast Keyboard Shortcuts

Cashiers can ring up sales without even touching the mouse! Here are the shortcuts:

| Key | What it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`F1`** | Jumps straight to the **Search Bar / Barcode input**. |
| **`F2`** | Opens the **Payment / Checkout** window. |
| **`F4`** | **Clears the bill** (cancels all items in current cart). |
| **`F5`** | Opens the **Customer Selection** window. |
| **`↓` (Arrow Down)** | Inside the quantity box, moves straight down to the **next item's quantity** and highlights the number so you can type immediately. |
| **`↑` (Arrow Up)** | Moves up to the **previous item's quantity**. |
| **`→` (Arrow Right)** | From the quantity box, jumps right into that item's **Discount box**. |
| **`←` (Arrow Left)** | From the discount box, jumps back to the **Quantity box**. |
| **`Enter`** | Confirms payment or moves to the next field. |
| **`Esc`** | Closes any open popup window. |

## 5. Managing Products (Adding & Updating Items)

*(Available to Managers and Store Owners)*

To view all products, click **Products** on the left menu.

### How to Add a New Product:
1. Click the **+ Add Product** button in the top right.
2. Fill in the simple details:
   - **Product Name**: (e.g. *Anchor Full Cream Milk 400g*).
   - **Category**: Select the category (e.g. *Dairy*, *Groceries*).
   - **Barcode / SKU**: Scan the barcode or type a unique item code.
   - **Cost Price**: What you paid to purchase this item (used to calculate your profit).
   - **Selling Price**: The price the customer pays.
   - **Base Inventory Unit**: Choose how this item is counted:
     - `Count`: for items sold in pieces, bottles, packets, boxes, cans, or pairs.
     - `Weight`: for items sold in grams or kilograms.
     - `Volume`: for items sold in milliliters or liters.
   - **Initial Stock**: How many you currently have on your shelves or in your warehouse.
   - **Low Stock Alert**: Enter a number (like `5`). When stock drops to 5, NexTill will alert you to restock.
3. Click **Save Product**.

### How to Edit or Change a Price:
1. Go to **Products**.
2. Find the item using the search bar.
3. Click the **Edit** (pencil) icon next to the item.
4. Update the price, name, or unit, and click **Save**.

## 6. Managing Stock & Restocking

*(Available to Managers and Store Owners)*

To check your stock, click **Inventory** on the left menu.

### 1. Checking Low Stock
At the top of the Inventory page, you can see items that are:
- **In Stock** (healthy quantity, shown in green).
- **Low Stock** (running out soon, shown in yellow).
- **Out of Stock** (empty, shown in red).

### 2. How to Restock When New Supplies Arrive
When new stock arrives at your shop:
1. Go to **Inventory**.
2. Find the product you want to restock and click **Restock**.
3. Choose the unit in which the new stock arrived:
   - For example: if you received `5 boxes` of soap, choose `box` and type `5`.
   - Or if you received `50 kg` of sugar, choose `kg` and type `50`.
4. Enter any supplier notes or invoice reference if you like.
5. Click **Confirm Restock**. NexTill will automatically add this to your existing inventory.

## 7. Customer Loyalty & Phone Numbers

NexTill allows you to remember your regular customers and reward them for shopping with you.

### How to Add or Select a Customer During Billing:
1. On the billing screen, click **Add Customer (F5)**.
2. Type their **Name** or **Phone Number**.
3. If they are already saved, click their name to select them.
4. If they are a new customer, click **+ Add Customer**, enter their name and phone number, and save.

### Customer Loyalty Points:
- Every time a customer makes a purchase, they automatically earn **Loyalty Points**.
- When they return, you can see their total points on the screen and give them special customer appreciation discounts.

## 8. Viewing Past Sales & Re-Printing Receipts

*(Available to Managers and Store Owners)*

Click **Sales** on the left menu to view your sales log.

### What You Can Do Here:
- **Search by Receipt / TXN Number**: Quickly find any past sale.
- **Filter by Date**: View all sales made today, this week, or during any custom date range.
- **Re-Print a Receipt**: If a customer comes back asking for a duplicate bill, click the **Receipt** button next to that transaction to view and print it again.
- **Process a Refund**:
  - If a customer returns a product, a manager can click **Refund**.
  - Confirm the refund amount.
  - The money is logged as returned and the item is put back into stock.

## 9. Business Reports & Dashboard

*(Available to Store Owners & Admins)*

Click **Dashboard** or **Reports** to understand how your business is performing without doing any manual math:

- **Today's Total Sales**: Total cash collected today.
- **Total Orders**: How many customers were served.
- **Total Profit**: Your net profit after subtracting item cost prices.

## 10. Store Settings & Staff Accounts

*(Available to Store Owners & Admins)*

### Store Settings (Click "Settings" in the menu):
Here you can personalize your store:
- **Store Name**: The name printed at the very top of the receipt (e.g. *SKR Supermarket*).
- **Store Address**: Printed under the store name (e.g. *Main Street, Kalmunai*).
- **Store Phone**: Your contact number on the receipt.
- **Receipt Footer Note**: A warm message to your shoppers (e.g. *"Thank you for shopping with us! Please come again."*).
- **Tax Rate**: Set your local sales tax percentage (leave `0` if you don't charge separate tax).

### Adding Staff Accounts (Click "Users" in the menu):
1. Click **+ Add User**.
2. Enter the employee's **Full Name**, **Username**, and a secure **Password**.
3. Choose their role:
   - **Cashier** for front-desk billing staff.
   - **Manager** for shop floor supervisors.
4. Click **Create User**. Each staff member will now have their own login, keeping your sales history accountable and secure.

## 11. Daily Store Checklist (Open to Close)

Here is a recommended daily routine for smooth store operations:

### ☀️ Morning Opening:
1. Turn on the computer and open NexTill.
2. Log in with your cashier or manager account.
3. Check **Inventory** to see if any essential items are running low.
4. Test one scan with your barcode scanner to ensure it is connected.
5. Make sure the receipt printer has enough paper.

### 🛒 During the Day:
1. Use **F1** to search or scan barcodes.
2. Enter quantities freely; use `↓` and `→` arrows for rapid typing.
3. Use **F2** to open payment, enter cash received, and hand back the exact change shown on screen.
4. Print receipts with one click.

### 🌙 Evening Closing:
1. Go to **Dashboard** or **Sales** to review the total cash collected for the day.
2. Compare the total cash in your cash drawer with the **Total Sales** on NexTill.
3. Click the user icon in the top right and click **Logout** to secure the terminal for the night.
